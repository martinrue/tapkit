Questions?

Who made this?

@martinrue created this because OpenAI gave him far too many resets not to. If you enjoy this, you may also like langmaxxing.com.

Does it work with the music I already listen to?

Yes. Tapkit plays its own sounds alongside whatever is playing on your Mac. Use your usual music app or website, summon an instrument, and join in.

Do I need to know how to play an instrument?

No. Start with a djembe or tambourine and tap to the beat. The piano, guitar, and other melodic instruments are there when you feel like exploring.

What is different about the browser demo?

The sounds, styles, and playing animations are the same. The Mac app adds floating windows, global shortcuts, custom keys, resizing, and the loop recorder. In the browser, the selected instrument responds whenever this page is focused.

Which Mac is this download for?

This preview is for Macs with Apple silicon. It is an unsigned prototype. The instruments and sounds are bundled with the app, so they work offline.

How do I install the unsigned Mac app?

Open the downloaded DMG, drag Tapkit into your Applications folder, then open it from there. This preview is unsigned and hasn’t been notarized by Apple.

If macOS blocks it because the developer can’t be verified, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security after trying to open Tapkit. Click Open Anyway, then confirm with Open. Only approve a download you trust. See Apple’s instructions.
