The people behind the sounds

Sounds & credits.

Good instruments start with good sounds. Tapkit uses the recordings below, together with its own electronic sounds and artwork. The licenses for these assets are independent of the app’s code.

Piano

Salamander Grand Piano v3 by Alexander Holm, distributed by Tone.js. Licensed under CC BY 3.0. Original recording · Author and license documentation · MP3 distribution.

The 21 MP3 files are unchanged. Playback trims quiet lead-in, normalizes levels, limits tails to seven seconds, adds short fades, and repitches samples to cover the keyboard. File details · Full license.

Djembe

Djembe bass, tone, tonpalo, slap by Michi Henning, recorded 3 July 2012. Source recording, licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0.

Tapkit extracts individual bass and rim strikes, converts them to mono WAV, trims and fades them, and extends the decay by 15%. Playback normalizes and lowers their pitches, with a stronger, deeper central hit. The adapted WAV files remain licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0. Exact edits and file details.

Handpan & guitar

Handpan: FreePats Hang tuned in D minor, performed by Mar, recorded by Gonzalo and Roberto, edited by Roberto. FreePats source. Guitar: FreePats Spanish classical guitar, recorded and created by Roberto. FreePats source. Both are dedicated under CC0 1.0.

Selected recordings are converted to mono WAV with trimmed lead-in and short fades. Guitar notes are repitched and layered into chords. The body tap is original synthesized wooden percussion by Tapkit, also CC0. Handpan file details · Guitar file details.

Trumpet, violin & marimba

VS Chamber Orchestra 2: Community Edition, recorded by Sam Gossner and Simon Dalzell; sample cutting by Elan Hickler / Soundemote. Source and CC0 license.

Recordings are converted, trimmed, faded, and mapped across playable registers. Trumpet uses articulated excerpts; violin uses smoothed, extended bow envelopes. Trumpet details · Violin details · Marimba details.

Tambourine & drum kit

Tambourine: Versilian Community Sample Library, Versilian Studios / Sam Gossner and contributors. Source and CC0 license. Drum kit: Virtuosity Drums, Versilian Studios / Karoryfer Samples, performed by Austin McMahon at Virtuosity Musical Instruments, Boston. Source and CC0 license.

Selected recordings are converted, trimmed, and faded into one-shots. Drum playback adjusts pitch and level for some voices. Tambourine details · Drum kit details.

Sampler

Twenty electronic one-shots are original offline synthesis by Tapkit, dedicated under CC0. Five vocal pads adapt these CC0 recordings:

  • hey.wav — matiasromero, 28 August 2007.
  • Yeah (Vocal) — leonschi13 / DJ N.D.5, 26 September 2011.
  • Let’s Go (male) — dastudiospr / Daniel Alvarez, 4 August 2020.

Vocals are cropped, trimmed, repitched, normalized, faded, and processed with echoes or chops. Sources and exact modifications.

Artwork & this demo

Instrument and hand illustrations were generated for Tapkit with OpenAI image generation. Sampler and marimba frames, their live pads and bars, and the Tapkit mark are original vector and code artwork. This site converts the PNG illustrations into smaller transparent WebP images; it uses the app’s sound engine, instrument geometry, and animations in a browser adapter.
