## Prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit / logo-brand. Edit the attached Wave logo concept for Tapkit. Preserve its extreme minimalism, single unified symbol, square canvas, centered horizontal composition, smooth rounded terminals, and bold uniform stroke weight. Make TWO requested changes: (1) INVERT the palette to a pure WHITE square background with a pure BLACK waveform. (2) Refine the simple single sine-wave squiggle into a recognizable musical AUDIO WAVEFORM: one continuous rounded black stroke with three smoothly connected oscillations, a small oscillation on the left, a taller main oscillation in the center, and a small oscillation on the right, like the compact amplitude envelope of one musical sound. Alternating rounded peaks and troughs, smooth flowing bends, clear wide negative spaces. The center is more energetic than the ends. Use only enough oscillations to read clearly as sound at a 16–20px menu-bar size. NOT an ECG heartbeat or lightning bolt: no sharp angular spikes. Keep the mark compact and unified, no separate equalizer bars, no musical-note glyph, no frame, no extra dots, no labels, no typography. The waveform should occupy roughly 70 percent of the square width and 35–45 percent of its height, with broad clean white margins. Pure monochrome flat vector-like edges: absolutely no gradients, texture, shadows, 3D, border, or surrounding mockup. This is ONE refined icon, not a sheet of variations. Its same black inner waveform must be usable alone as a transparent menu-bar icon.
